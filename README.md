# Ria Money Transfer Application

A mobile remittance application designed for sending international money transfers quickly, securely, and transparently across 190+ countries and territories.

## Overview
The **Ria Money Transfer** application allows users to initiate cross-border payments directly from their smartphones. Backed by over 35 years of industry experience, the app connects senders with a global payout network offering real-time tracking, upfront exchange rates, and flexible delivery options.

## Key Features
* **Global Reach:** Send money to over 190 countries and territories worldwide.
* **Multiple Payout Methods:**
  * Direct bank deposits
  * Cash pickup at hundreds of thousands of partner locations globally
  * Digital and mobile wallets (e.g., Yape, Nequi, MonCash, Pago Móvil, depending on destination)
  * Home delivery in select regions
* **Flexible Funding Options:** Pay for transfers using a bank account, debit card, or credit card.
* **Transparent Pricing:** View exact fees and exchange rates before confirming any transaction.
* **Real-Time Tracking:** Monitor transfer status step-by-step using a unique reference number or PIN.
* **Repeat Transfers:** Save recipient profiles and payment details for faster recurring transactions.
* **Additional Services:** Access mobile top-ups and bill payment features (availability varies by country).

## Supported Platforms
* **iOS:** Available via the Apple App Store
* **Android:** Available via the Google Play Store

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
* A compatible iOS or Android smartphone
* A valid government-issued ID (for identity verification / KYC compliance)
* An active payment method (bank account, debit card, or credit card)

### Quick Start Workflow
1. **Install:** Download the Ria Money Transfer app from your device's app store.
2. **Register & Verify:** Create an account and complete the required identity verification process.
3. **Configure Transfer:** Select the destination country, enter the transfer amount, and choose a payout method.
4. **Add Recipient:** Input the recipient's contact and payout details (or select a saved recipient).
5. **Pay & Confirm:** Select your funding source, review the exchange rate and total fee, and submit.
6. **Track:** Follow the transfer progress in real time until funds are delivered or picked up.
